About This Recipe

This is the cottage cheese chocolate mousse that broke Pinterest in 2024 — and rightfully so. The premise sounds absurd: blend cottage cheese with cocoa powder and get something that passes as elegant chocolate mousse. The reality is that a high-speed blender turns cottage cheese completely smooth and silky, erasing every trace of the curds and leaving a creamy, dense base that happens to be loaded with casein protein. Add Dutch-process cocoa, vanilla, and a drizzle of honey, and in 5 minutes you have something richer than many traditional mousses — at 185 calories and 20 grams of protein per serving. No eggs. No heavy cream. No cooking. No chilling required (though 30 minutes in the refrigerator gives it a beautiful scoopable firmness). The dessert that health-conscious Pinterest users called "the cheat code."

Ingredients

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. 1

    Blend the cottage cheese until perfectly smooth

    This step is the entire secret to the recipe. Add the cottage cheese to a high-speed blender (Vitamix, Ninja, Blendtec, or similar). Blend on high speed for 60–90 seconds, stopping once to scrape down the sides with a spatula. You are looking for a texture that is completely smooth, silky, and homogeneous — like thick sour cream or Greek yogurt. There should be absolutely zero visible curd texture remaining. If you can still see or feel any graininess, keep blending. A regular blender may take 2–3 minutes to fully smooth cottage cheese; a powerful high-speed blender needs about 60–90 seconds. Do not rush this step — any residual curd texture will make the mousse grainy.

  2. 2

    Add the chocolate and flavorings

    With the blended cottage cheese still in the blender, add the unsweetened cocoa powder (Dutch-process gives a deeper, less acidic chocolate flavor than natural cocoa), honey or maple syrup, vanilla extract, and salt. Blend on high for another 30 seconds until everything is completely incorporated and the mousse is uniformly dark, smooth, and glossy. Taste the mousse: if you want more chocolate intensity, add another tablespoon of cocoa and blend again. If it needs more sweetness, add another teaspoon of honey. For an even richer result, add 2 tablespoons of melted dark chocolate (60–70% cocoa) and blend briefly — this adds luxurious depth and genuine chocolate fat that makes the mousse taste almost indistinguishable from the real thing.

  3. 3

    Adjust texture and serve

    The mousse should be thick and spoonable — roughly the consistency of a thick pudding or loose hummus. If it's too thick to blend or too dense for your taste, add 1 tablespoon of milk (any type) and blend briefly. Spoon into two serving glasses or ramekins. For a soft mousse, serve immediately. For a firmer, more traditional mousse texture, cover and refrigerate for 30 minutes — the cottage cheese protein sets as it chills, giving a scoopable consistency similar to set chocolate pudding. Top each serving with a swirl of whipped cream applied with a piping bag or spoon, chocolate shavings scraped from a block of dark chocolate with a vegetable peeler, and a fresh raspberry or two.

Pro Tips

🏆 Getting the Best Results
  • Use full-fat cottage cheese. 4% milkfat cottage cheese produces the richest, creamiest result. Low-fat works but is thinner; fat-free produces a watery mousse.
  • Use Dutch-process cocoa. Dutch-process cocoa is darker, less acidic, and more chocolatey than natural cocoa. It makes a dramatic difference in flavor. Valrhona, Droste, and King Arthur are excellent brands.
  • Small-curd cottage cheese blends smoother. Small-curd varieties need less blending time to fully smooth than large-curd.
  • Add a tablespoon of peanut butter for a chocolate-peanut butter version that tastes like a Reese's cup with 22g protein.
  • For a vegan version: Use plant-based cottage cheese (Kite Hill or Good Planet) and maple syrup. The texture is slightly different but still genuinely impressive.

Flavor Variations

🔄 5 Flavor Variations
  • Peanut Butter Chocolate: Add 2 tbsp natural peanut butter. 22g protein. Tastes like a Reese's cup.
  • Mocha: Add 1 tsp instant espresso powder with the cocoa. Deep, complex coffee-chocolate flavor.
  • Mint Chocolate: Add ¼ tsp peppermint extract. Top with crushed dark chocolate mint candies.
  • Mexican Hot Chocolate: Add ½ tsp cinnamon and a pinch of cayenne with the cocoa for warmth and spice.
  • Protein Powder Boost: Add 1 scoop chocolate protein powder for 35–40g protein per serving. Add 2–3 tbsp milk to adjust consistency.
