Marry Me Chicken — Creamy Sun-Dried Tomato Chicken

⏱ Prep: 10 min🔥 Cook: 25 min ⏰ Total: 35 min👥 Serves: 4 📊 Easy ⭐ 4.9(3,241 reviews)

About This Recipe

Marry Me Chicken is the recipe that broke the internet — and for very good reason. Golden, pan-seared chicken breasts nestle into the most luxurious, velvety cream sauce studded with chewy sun-dried tomatoes, fragrant garlic, freshly grated parmesan, and a hint of red pepper heat. It's the kind of dish that stops conversation mid-sentence. The kind that makes people put down their phones and actually eat. Named for the wave of proposals allegedly inspired by this exact dish, Marry Me Chicken delivers full fine-dining flavour with a single skillet and 35 minutes. Serve it over pasta, mashed potatoes, or crusty bread and prepare to become very, very popular.

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless skinless chicken breasts
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • Salt & black pepper
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • ½ cup sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • ½ cup grated parmesan
  • 1 tsp red pepper flakes
  • Fresh basil to garnish

Instructions

  1. 1Season chicken Coat both sides with Italian seasoning, garlic powder, paprika, salt, and pepper.
  2. 2Sear Heat oil over medium-high. Sear chicken 4–5 min per side until golden. Remove and set aside.
  3. 3Build sauce Sauté garlic 1 min. Add sun-dried tomatoes 2 min. Pour in broth, scraping up bits.
  4. 4Add cream Stir in heavy cream and parmesan. Simmer 3–4 min until slightly thickened.
  5. 5Finish chicken Return chicken. Spoon sauce over. Simmer 5–7 min until 165°F internal temp.
  6. 6Serve Garnish with fresh basil and red pepper flakes. Serve immediately over pasta or potatoes.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. 1

    Season the chicken generously

    Pat the chicken breasts dry with paper towels — this is essential for a proper golden sear. Mix the Italian seasoning, garlic powder, smoked paprika, salt, and plenty of black pepper, then coat both sides of each breast. Don't be shy with the seasoning — you're building flavour from the very first step. If your chicken breasts are thick (over 1 inch), pound them slightly with a meat mallet for even cooking.

  2. 2

    Sear the chicken until deeply golden

    Heat olive oil in a large skillet or cast iron pan over medium-high heat until shimmering. Place the chicken breasts smooth-side down and do not move them — let them develop a proper golden crust for 4–5 minutes. Flip and cook another 4–5 minutes until the second side is equally golden. The chicken won't be cooked through at this point — that's fine, it finishes in the sauce. Transfer to a plate and tent loosely with foil.

  3. 3

    Build the sauce base

    In the same skillet (don't wipe it — those browned bits are pure flavour), reduce heat to medium and add the minced garlic. Sauté for 1 minute until fragrant, stirring constantly so it doesn't burn. Add the chopped sun-dried tomatoes and cook for another 2 minutes, pressing them gently with a wooden spoon to release their concentrated tomato oils into the pan. The whole kitchen should smell incredible right now.

  4. 4

    Deglaze and add cream

    Pour in the chicken broth and use a wooden spoon to scrape up every bit of the golden fond from the bottom of the pan — that's where all the flavour lives. Bring to a simmer, then pour in the heavy cream and stir to combine. Add the freshly grated parmesan and red pepper flakes. Stir until the parmesan melts completely. Simmer for 3–4 minutes until the sauce thickens slightly and coats the back of a spoon.

  5. 5

    Return chicken and finish in sauce

    Nestle the seared chicken breasts back into the creamy sauce. Spoon the sauce over the tops generously. Reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and simmer for 5–7 minutes until the chicken registers 165°F on an instant-read thermometer. The sauce will thicken further as the chicken finishes cooking. If the sauce gets too thick, add a splash of chicken broth to loosen.

  6. 6

    Garnish and serve immediately

    Remove from heat. Tear fresh basil leaves and scatter generously over the chicken and sauce. Finish with an extra pinch of red pepper flakes and a crack of black pepper. Serve straight from the skillet for drama — it looks stunning. Spoon the sauce over pasta, mashed potatoes, or into bowls with crusty bread. This is the moment people fall in love.

Pro Tips

🏆 Chef's Tips for Perfect Results
  • Use sun-dried tomatoes in oil. The oil-packed variety are softer and more flavourful than dry-packed. Drain them well but save the oil — a teaspoon stirred into the sauce at the end adds incredible depth.
  • Freshly grate your parmesan. Pre-shredded parmesan contains anti-caking agents that prevent it from melting smoothly. A block of real Parmigiano-Reggiano grated fresh makes the sauce silky rather than grainy.
  • Don't overcrowd the pan. If searing more than 4 chicken breasts, do it in batches. Crowding steams the chicken instead of searing it and you lose that golden crust that makes all the difference.
  • Pound thick breasts. Uneven chicken breasts cook unevenly. Place them in a zip-lock bag and pound to an even ¾-inch thickness for perfectly cooked chicken every time.

Delicious Variations

🔄 Ways to Switch It Up
  • Marry Me Chicken Pasta: Cook 12 oz of spaghetti or rigatoni and toss directly in the finished sauce. Slice the chicken on top. Shower with extra parmesan — this is the version that actually makes people propose.
  • Baked version: Sear the chicken on the stovetop, make the sauce, then transfer to a 375°F oven for 15–20 minutes until cooked through. Great for entertaining larger groups.
  • With spinach: Stir a big handful of baby spinach into the sauce at the very end. It wilts in 30 seconds and adds gorgeous colour and nutrition.
  • Slow cooker version: Season and brown the chicken, pour everything into a slow cooker, and cook on low for 4–5 hours. Stir the parmesan in during the last 30 minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions

The name comes from the idea that this dish is so delicious it could inspire a marriage proposal. It went viral online when countless people shared stories of their partners proposing after eating it. Whether or not the proposal comes, the recipe genuinely delivers restaurant-quality flavour with minimal effort — and that alone is worth celebrating.
Boneless skinless chicken thighs actually work even better — they're juicier, more forgiving, and nearly impossible to overcook. Use the same timing (4–5 minutes per side to sear, then 5–7 minutes in the sauce). The richer flavour of thighs pairs beautifully with the creamy sauce.
You can use half-and-half for a lighter sauce, but the texture will be thinner. Coconut cream makes an excellent dairy-free substitute — it adds a subtle sweetness that works surprisingly well with the sun-dried tomatoes. Avoid milk alone as it won't thicken properly and may curdle over high heat.
Store chicken and sauce together in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days. Reheat gently in a skillet over low heat with a splash of chicken broth to loosen the sauce. Avoid high heat which causes cream sauces to break and separate. The flavours deepen overnight — leftovers are genuinely excellent.
